# Onslaught Lore

- **Status:** live entry guide for the packaged offline Lore reader. **This is
  the only file that belongs in `lore-book/`** — the pack builder rejects any
  other path under this directory, and the articles themselves live once under
  [`lore/`](../lore/_index.md) with no tracked mirror. The link list below is
  also the reading order: the builder derives each document's position from it,
  so moving a line here moves the document in the reader.
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-01
- **Summary:** the front door — what this is, the shelves, and where to start.

In 2003 a studio in Guildford shipped a game about a walking machine that could
turn into a jet. The reviews were good and the sales were not, and on 2 October
that year Lost Toys Ltd closed and made everyone redundant. It had existed for
four years.

This is what is left of it: the war the game invented, the people who built it,
and the things they only said out loud afterwards. Some of it comes from the game
itself. Some comes from a post-mortem its lead programmer wrote the year it
shipped. Some comes from its developers turning up in a Discord two decades later
to answer questions nobody had asked them since — the original directors had not
been in regular contact for twenty years when one of them joined in April 2025.

They named the planet Allium, which is the onion and garlic family, and kept
going from there.

Where an article disagrees with a measurement taken from the game, the
measurement wins and the article is the side that gets corrected. That has
happened more than once; what those articles used to say is kept out of your way.
