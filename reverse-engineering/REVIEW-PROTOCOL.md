# Review protocol

Status: active — optional independent review with local verification
Last updated: 2026-09-06
Summary: use reviewers for concrete questions; evidence and existing gates decide the result.
Evidence: SOURCE — current maintainer instructions in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)
own scope and preservation. Reviewer reports are fallible input.

## Choose a useful review

Use independent review when a consequential change or uncertain finding benefits
from another reader. Give the reviewer the actual artifacts, a bounded question,
and the applicable owner guides. Choose depth and parallelism for that question;
no model matrix, reviewer quota or per-generation ceremony is required.

Prefer the active harness's native reviewers and strongest available setting.
External reviewers are optional. Verify their available tools and model options
when needed instead of copying old provider commands or model identifiers.
Do not automatically launch a provider, spend money or disclose material outside
the user's authorized scope.

Reviewers stay read-only. They may identify a repair but never perform a destructive
action. An authorized implementation task with an isolated writing worktree is a
separate role. The integration owner preserves unrelated work and owns admission
of any change.

## Admit findings through evidence

Reproduce load-bearing findings with primary bytes, source, a controlled observation
or the smallest existing check that could falsify them. Review the instrument as
well as its conclusion: a parser, reducer or exporter can manufacture false confidence.
Agreement between models is not independent measurement.

A review does not replace campaign parent/delta/receipt/reducer/replay gates,
Ghidra preservation and readback, save/patch recovery checks or native runtime
acceptance. It cannot authorize a new campaign, capture, database mutation or
product claim. Follow the owning guide and current user scope.

Stop adding reviewers once the next useful step is the mechanical falsifier.
A timeout, empty output, authentication prompt or partial response is an incomplete
review, never success. Do not restart obsolete review matrices to fill empty cells.

## Preserve the useful record

Use the existing review owner under ignored `local-lab/`. Retain the exact prompt,
complete report, actual model/effort, role, outcome and timing when available;
mark missing metadata unavailable instead of inventing it. For an external command,
retain its direct output and exit status. Record the primary's reproduced findings
and dispositions in that same record; do not create another status framework.

Keep credentials, raw retail payloads, decompiler bodies and private evidence out
of hosted prompts unless the specific disclosure is authorized and permitted.
Respect [rebuild provenance](../rebuild/PROVENANCE.md). Historical review outputs
remain evidence with their original limits; old instructions and provider recipes
remain in Git history and do not select current work.
